# DO NOT DRIVE

The independently generated routes in this directory are not approved for
motion.

Field feedback reported sidewalk departure on downhill curb segments. A
bilateral audit found 40 one-sided clearance violations in the previously
reported final route. A denser 0.2 m safety-band scan found additional
physically narrow stations. Recentring attempts exposed further bottlenecks
and did not converge to a route whose complete wheel envelope could be proven
inside the sidewalk.

The follower now refuses startup when either:

- a non-endpoint band station has less than 0.45 m on either side of the route
  centre; or
- a chord between adjacent band stations leaves the band.

Do not bypass these gates or run with safety policies disabled.
